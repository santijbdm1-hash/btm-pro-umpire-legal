Privacy notice

Status: Draft. Effective date to be set after approval.

This notice describes the standalone BTM PRO Umpire app, part of the BTM Pro family. It is derived from the umpire experience in BTM Pro (Score Broadcasting System), or SBS. SBS and its online services may have their own notices.

Who to contact

Publisher: BTM Pro, Colombia. Privacy contact: santi@btmpro.net. You can contact us about this notice or a privacy request.

Information entered in the app

The app lets an umpire enter player names, umpire profile names and match details. It records scores, match events and results, and stores preferences such as language, theme and scoring rules. This information is used to run and resume matches and display their history.

Storage, backup and deletion

Matches and settings for the standalone local match flow are stored on the device. The app keeps matches until you remove them or clear the app’s data. Android system backup is enabled; depending on your device and backup settings, app data may also be copied or restored by your platform provider. Uninstalling or clearing app data may remove local records. Copies shared or backed up separately may remain with the recipient or backup provider.

To remove a match, open History and use the available removal control. To remove the app’s local data, use Android’s app storage settings; doing so removes all local app data. Contact us if you need help with a privacy request. If an organiser controls a match, contact the organiser about their own copies and decisions.

Sharing and other services

If you choose to share or copy a result, the selected sharing app, clipboard or recipient receives the information you choose to share. Those services operate under their own privacy notices. Opening an external BTM Pro link takes you to a separate website.

For the current standalone local match flow, the app does not require an SBS server to create or score a match.

Other people’s information and children

When you enter someone else’s name or share a result, make sure you have an appropriate basis to do so and follow the rules that apply to your event. Information about children may require additional protections or permissions under applicable law.

Contact and website information

If you contact us, we use your message and contact details to respond and retain them for as long as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Our email and website hosting providers may process technical information necessary to deliver those services, subject to their own terms and policies.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information, including access, correction or deletion. Contact the privacy address above; we will respond as required by applicable law. If the information is held only on your device, we may need to direct you or the event organiser to the local record. In Colombia, you may also contact the Superintendencia de Industria y Comercio concerning your data protection rights.

Security and changes

Protect your device and take care when sharing results. No device or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure. We may update this notice as the app changes and will provide the current version through the app and its public policy link.
